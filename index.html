📝 White Paper · 2024 · Published

Journalism and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

This white paper was born from a simple question: what happens when a law designed to protect privacy collides with the press's duty to inform? India's DPDPA 2023 is one of the most consequential pieces of digital legislation in the country's history — but its implications for journalism hadn't been adequately explored.

The paper examines consent frameworks, data minimisation requirements, and the right to erasure through the lens of investigative journalism, source protection, and public interest disclosure. It evaluates whether the Act's exemptions for journalistic activity are sufficient, and proposes a framework that respects both individual privacy and democratic accountability.

Writing this paper taught me something that no textbook could: that the most important problems live in the spaces between disciplines.
